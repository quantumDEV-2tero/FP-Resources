Terms & Data Privacy

Conditions of Utilisation

Last updated: September 2026

1. Purpose of the Platform

FP Library is an independent, student-built resource library created to help Physics students at Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Larache share notes, exercises, exams, reports, and other academic documents. It is not an official service of the university.

2. Acceptable Use

By using this site you agree to:

  • Only upload content you have the right to share (your own notes, or materials you are permitted to redistribute).
  • Use the documents and community features for personal academic purposes only.
  • Not upload harmful, offensive, defamatory, or illegal content.
  • Not attempt to impersonate another student, professor, or staff member.
  • Not use the messaging or review features to harass, spam, or misrepresent facts about other users or professors.

3. Accounts

You are responsible for the security of your account and for any activity carried out under it. Accounts may be suspended if these conditions are violated.

4. Content Ownership & Removal

Uploaders retain ownership of the documents they share. FP Library may remove any content, review, or account at its discretion, for example in response to a copyright or conduct complaint.

5. No Warranty

Documents and information (including professor reviews) are provided "as is" by the community. FP Library does not guarantee their accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any particular exam or course requirement.

6. Changes to These Conditions

These conditions may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the new conditions.

Data Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account data: your email address and any profile details you choose to add (name, avatar, bio).
  • Content you share: uploaded documents, professor reviews, and messages sent through the platform.
  • Usage data: basic interactions with the site needed to make features like messaging and the student directory work.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To let other students find you in the directory and message you, if you choose to appear there.
  • To display your reviews and uploaded content to other students.
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse.

3. Who Can See Your Data

Your profile information (name, avatar, bio) and any reviews you post are visible to other logged-in students. Your email address is not publicly displayed unless you choose to share it in a message.

4. Data Storage

Data is stored using the platform's backend services. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but as an independent student project, FP Library cannot guarantee the same level of security as a commercial service.

5. Your Rights

  • Access: you can review the personal data associated with your account at any time via "My Profile".
  • Correction: you can edit your profile information whenever you like.
  • Deletion: you may request that your account and associated data be deleted by contacting the site administrators.
  • Withdrawal: you can stop using the platform at any time; you are not obligated to keep an account.

6. Contact

For any question about your data or these conditions, please reach out through the upload/contact form linked in the navigation bar.

SYSTEM INFORMATION

Built by students. Open to everyone.

FP Library is an independent, student-built public resource website created to help Physics students at Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Larache find, organize, and share useful academic materials.

This website is not officially owned, operated, approved, or affiliated with Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Larache, Université Abdelmalek Essaâdi, or any university administration.

The platform is made by students for students. Its purpose is to support learning through shared course notes, summaries, exercises, tutorials, documents, and other educational resources related to Physics.

Students may contribute by uploading and sharing documents that could help other students with their studies. Uploaded materials should be relevant, respectful, and used for educational purposes.

The website is publicly accessible on the internet. Anyone may visit and view the available resources, while the main intended community is Physics students at FP Larache.

Important notice: FP Library is an independent student initiative. It does not represent the university or speak on behalf of its staff, departments, or administration.
